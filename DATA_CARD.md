# Data Card

This public repository contains official Eurostat source manifests, raw official files, processed modeling panels, sample files, and lightweight result tables for a course/research prototype.

Locked public facts:

- Stage 1: 553 panel rows, 544 modeling rows, 36 geo groups.
- Stage 2: 5,814 modeling rows, 36 geo groups, 50 industries.
- Stage 2 source chain: 17 verified Eurostat official files, 12,770,332 profiled source rows, 856,880 feature-filtered rows, aggregated to a 5,814-row modeling panel.

The correct wording is that tens-of-millions-scale official source rows were filtered and aggregated into modeling panels. They were not directly used as training samples.
